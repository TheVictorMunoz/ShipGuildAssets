# ShipGuild Brand Guidelines

## The marks

**SG emblem.** The primary mark. A crew of three standing on the bow of a ship, the bow formed by the S and G, a keel below, inside a ring. Use it for avatars, app icons, stickers and anywhere the brand needs to read at a glance.

**ShipGuild wordmark.** Custom italic lettering. Use it as artwork. Do not retype it in a font.

**SHIP mark.** The "ship it" badge: chrome letters with an upward trajectory arrow. Use it for launch moments, campaigns, the SHIP program and merch. It is secondary to the emblem and should not replace it as the main logo.

**Lockups.** Emblem + wordmark + tagline. Use the horizontal lockup for site headers and decks, the stacked lockup for square formats and print.

## Color

| Name | Hex | RGB | Use |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |

| Lime | `#C8EE10` | 200 238 16 | Core brand color, accents, buttons |
| Signal | `#93D106` | 147 209 6 | Gradient base, secondary accents |
| Deep | `#4E9A00` | 78 154 0 | Shadows, keel facet, accents on light |
| Forest | `#24470A` | 36 71 10 | Logo color on white and light backgrounds |
| Void | `#050705` | 5 7 5 | Primary background |
| Panel | `#0D130C` | 13 19 12 | Cards and raised surfaces |
| Mist | `#EAF7E2` | 234 247 226 | Light text on dark, glass highlight |

Brand gradient (emblem, wordmark): Volt to Lime to Signal, top to bottom.

ShipGuild is a dark-first brand. Lime on Void is the default. On light backgrounds, switch the marks to Forest or black. Never put lime marks on white: the contrast is 1.3:1.

Contrast checks for text:
- Lime `#C8EE10` on Void: 15.1:1, fine at any size.
- Mist on Void: 18.2:1.
- Forest on white: 10.6:1.
- Deep `#4E9A00` on white: 3.5:1, large text and accents only.

## Type

- **Tagline and labels:** [Michroma](https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Michroma) (SIL Open Font License), uppercase, tracked wide (about 0.4 to 0.55 em). The tagline sets "TO" and "BY" in Lime.
- **Wordmark:** custom lettering, use the SVG.
- **Body copy (suggested):** a clean sans such as Inter or the system UI stack. Keep Michroma for short labels; it is too wide for paragraphs.

Tagline: **BUILT TO SCALE. DRIVEN BY COMMUNITY.**

## Clear space and minimum size

- Clear space around any mark: at least the height of one crew head (about 10% of the emblem width) on every side.
- Emblem minimum: 24 px on screen, 10 mm in print. Below 48 px use `shipguild-favicon-small.svg` (no ring, no crew), which stays legible down to 16 px.
- Wordmark minimum: 80 px wide on screen, 25 mm in print.
- SHIP mark minimum: 120 px wide. Use the `flat` or mono versions below 300 px; the glow turns to mush when small.

## Do

- Use the supplied files as they are.
- Use the mono versions (white, black, forest) for single-color printing, embroidery, engraving and watermarks.
- Put the glow SHIP mark and the color emblem on Void or on dark photography.

## Don't

- Don't stretch, skew, rotate or re-slant the marks.
- Don't recolor outside the palette or swap the gradient direction.
- Don't add extra drop shadows, bevels or outlines to the emblem.
- Don't put the color marks on busy mid-tone backgrounds; use mono white or black there.
- Don't rebuild the wordmark from a font.
- Don't rearrange the crew, the S and G, or the keel inside the emblem.
